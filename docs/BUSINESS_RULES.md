# Business workflows (approved discovery)

## CRM / sales
Customer source records Sales/social channel and platform/referral, who acquired the customer, account manager, meetings and notes. Leads → requirements → proposal (versions, scope, price, currency, installments, tax inclusive/exclusive) → approval policy by value/discount → contract without rekeying. Approved proposal data is snapshotted into contract; document changes don't mutate signed artifacts.

## Projects
Contract → project with PM and participants from either legal company → UI/UX approval → development → final delivery → customer bug-discovery period and fixes. Custom milestones link contractual payment installments. PM alone certifies milestone for due payment; system prepares an invoice draft; Finance reviews and issues it. Payments are manually registered and can be partial. Tasks have flexible statuses, priority, subtasks, comments, attachments, dependencies, review and QA, workload and time tracking; time entries require owning PM approval.

## HR
Mixed office/remote, fixed/flexible schedules. First work-task action auto-checks in without auto-starting a time timer; checkout manual with reminder, estimated auto closure distinctly marked for review. Leave request → line manager → HR final; leave accrual, carryover and exceptions configurable by employer. Mixed fixed/hourly/project/freelance payroll: HR prepare → Finance review → Management approve → Finance records disbursement. Salary advances repay lump sum or installments; approvals tiered by amount. Sales commissions are configurable, default paid upon actual collection, and can recur. Employee performance reviews include PM feedback, direct manager final review and HR recording.

## Finance / accounting
Independent company ledgers, bank accounts, invoices, taxes, multi-currency and FX snapshot. Operational finance plus external accountant per company. Double-entry posting, reconciliation, cash flow, P&L, balance sheet and project profitability. Egypt issues Italy monthly services invoices and optional project-specific invoices; intercompany balances eliminated only for consolidation. No silent modification of posted financial data.

## SaaS / maintenance / hosting
Shared/dedicated SaaS with product plans, custom prices, recurring billing and auto-renewal unless canceled. Cancellation stops service immediately subject to an individually configured penalty; finance reviews penalty invoice. Contracts support SLA, business hours/24h, priority, escalation, limited/unlimited maintenance hours, flexible expiry and rollover. Support comes via WhatsApp/email/calls/manual tickets; tickets can link to tasks. Infrastructure tracks servers/domains/vendors, costs, renewal reminders and owner; initial vendor payment entries are manual.

## Administration
Central + independent company management, flexible approval thresholds, limited external accountant portal within internal ERP, role-based dashboards, notification inbox and important email notices. No customer login.
